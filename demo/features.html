Chapters, seeking, captions, mini player

Run bun run build, serve the repository root (bunx serve .) and open /demo/features.html. Add ?src=/path/to/video.mp4 to try your own file in both players.

1. Honest bar you can seek, inline chapters, two caption languages, mini player

bar="linear" makes the bar a slider: drag it, tap it, or focus it and use the arrow keys, Page Up/Down, Home and End. The chapter button next to the time opens the list. The captions button offers English and Dutch, and remembers the choice. Press play, then scroll down: the video follows in the bottom right corner.

Scroll past the video

While it plays with sound, the player floats in the corner. Its place above keeps its size, so this text does not jump. The arrow on the mini player scrolls back; the cross returns it and keeps it there for this page view.

2. Rapid bar with chapter marks from a WebVTT file, one captions file

The default bar runs ahead early and is never seekable. Its chapter marks sit where the curve puts each time, so the fill reaches a mark exactly when the chapter starts. Chapters come from chapters.vtt; the last cue there starts after the video ends and is ignored. With one captions file the button is a plain on/off switch.

The second player floats top left, 280 pixels wide, and also follows while paused (mini-when="always").
